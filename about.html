About Prescope™

Built from the work.
Not around it.

Prescope wasn't designed by engineers observing product teams from the outside. It was built by someone who has worked directly inside those teams — as a Business Analyst, Product Owner, Product Manager, and Technical Delivery Lead — across some of the most complex enterprise environments in the world.

The problem that wouldn't go away

Across 15+ years of enterprise delivery — spanning financial services, insurance, retail, healthcare IT, federal government, payments, and platform engineering — one pattern repeated itself regardless of industry, team size, or methodology.

Stakeholders bring messy, broad, vague requests to planning sessions. Teams decompose them into stories immediately. Then the stories get contested in refinement, debated in sprint planning, revised mid-sprint, and eventually rewritten from scratch — because the underlying request was never properly classified or scoped before the work began.

"The problem isn't writing user stories. It's knowing when you're ready to write them."

The root cause isn't poor execution. It's a missing step. No one stops to ask: what kind of work request is this, really? Is this an Epic spanning multiple systems and teams? A high-level initiative that needs features defined first? Or is it actually a Feature — specific enough that stories make sense right now?

Every AI tool built for backlog and requirements work makes this mistake: they assume every input is already a Feature, and generate stories immediately. Prescope was built to fix that — not by making story generation faster, but by making the entire process more correct from the start.

What makes it different

The classify-first approach isn't a design preference. It's a methodology decision grounded in how experienced delivery professionals actually think about scope. The six classification levels — Epic, High-Level Initiative, Initiative/Feature Group, Feature, User Story, Discovery Needed — map directly to the mental model that senior BAs, POs, PMs, and Project Managers apply before they write a single requirement.

The domain contexts — ITSM, SaaS, Retail, Healthcare — aren't decorative labels. Each one carries a specific set of personas, platforms, regulatory constraints, and delivery archetypes that fundamentally change what good requirements look like. An incident lifecycle workflow in a regulated financial institution looks nothing like a checkout optimization in an ecommerce platform. Prescope knows the difference and reflects it in every output.

The process flow diagrams with business rules and data rules exist because there was a real gap: teams were generating stories without any artifact showing how the solution would actually operate at runtime. Every experienced BA knows this artifact matters. Prescope generates it automatically, without requiring a separate Visio or Lucidchart session.

What we believe

🎯
Classify before you generate
Every request deserves to be understood before it's decomposed. Classification isn't a gate — it's the step that makes every downstream artifact faster, more accurate, and more defensible in refinement.
🧠
AI drafts, humans own
Every output is a starting point, not a final answer. The BA, PO, or PM reviewing, editing, and approving the output is the product. The tool removes the blank page — the professional provides the judgment.
🔧
Domain context is not optional
A NOC analyst managing incident lifecycles and a product manager optimizing a checkout funnel share the same methodology — but their deliverables, personas, and constraints are entirely different. Generic AI treats them the same. Prescope doesn't.
📋
Governance is not overhead
Status tracking, process flows, business rules, and exportable artifacts aren't bureaucracy. They're what separates a backlog that gets signed off from one that gets argued about in every planning meeting.
🔍
Discovery before decomposition
Some requests aren't ready to be decomposed at all. Recognizing when a stakeholder need requires discovery — not stories — is one of the most valuable things an experienced BA does. Prescope is the only tool that makes this a first-class output.
📐
Progressive over immediate
Epic to initiative to feature to story — each level earned, not assumed. Progressive decomposition isn't just a workflow. It's a discipline that produces work artifacts teams can actually deliver against.
